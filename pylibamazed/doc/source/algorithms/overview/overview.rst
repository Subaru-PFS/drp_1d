Overview
========

:term:`AMAZED` is a C++ spectroscopic redshift-determination library. It is used to determine the redshift of large samples of objects in an automatic way. It allows the computation of the redshift, the emission-line fluxes and their associated errors, the reliability of the redshift determination, the PDF, the estimated continuum, and the template classification. The algorithms have been developed to account for the emission and absorption properties of the objects, as well as the ISM and IGM absorption.
